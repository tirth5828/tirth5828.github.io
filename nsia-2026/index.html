Preserving Manifold Structure in Landmark Coresets: Morse-Seeded Sampling on kNN Graphs

Tirth Joshi Best Poster

NSIA 2026 (Network Science Informs AI), a satellite of NetSci 2026 · Boston, June 2026

YU News

A large set of points is often summarised by a small set of landmarks. Choosing landmarks only for even coverage can ignore the shape the data lies on. This poster seeds the landmarks from a density potential on the k-nearest-neighbour graph, read as a Morse function, so the choice starts from the structure of the graph.

The potential

Φ(i) = ‖xi − xi,k‖2 where xi,k is the k-th nearest neighbour of xi. A lower Φ(i) means a locally denser region.
