AI harnesses for a fast-moving product team

uBuyFirst · AI Engineer Intern · 2026

I joined a large, highly qualified team that ships fast, and built the AI harness: the setup that lets AI agents do real work across the product's systems, for the developers and for the customers.

Two harnesses

For developers, an agentic setup that works across many systems under the project's own rules. Shared team rules sit at the top. Each role, such as development, operations or SEO, has its own agent configuration, and a template turns a new role into a configuration of its own. Every configuration ships as a versioned, packaged release, so a move from v1 to v2 is a deliberate step. It also streamlined onboarding: someone new learns the system through the harness and gets productive quickly.

For customers, the AI works through the product's public MCP connector. Through it, an assistant can manage a user's saved searches, item filters and their folders, the blocklist, notification channels and recent alerts, and it can search listings. A public command-line tool covers bulk changes.

Also

I also worked on the desktop apps, and now on the product's new web app with the team.

What I take from it

  1. A clean use of AI: a harness and agentic systems that raise a whole team's productivity, not a demo.
  2. How to work in a very high-pace team, and keep up with it.
