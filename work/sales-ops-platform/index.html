An AI sales and operations platform, built solo

A&A Coatings · AI Engineer Intern · 2026

A real application, used every day at a real company. I built it on my own, and it replaced a sales inbox read by hand, a spreadsheet schedule and quotes buried in mail threads. This page is about how it is built and what it took.

What it replaced

  • Sales inbox read by hand→Now: AI classifies each email, extracts the specs from RFQs and drawings, and drafts a reply that a person reviews and sends
  • Spreadsheet schedule→Now: Live production schedule
  • Quotes in mail threads→Now: Quote workspace and deal pipeline
  • Manual reports→Now: Built-in analytics

The intake pipeline

Every inbound email takes the same path. A classifier decides what kind of email it is. For a request for quote, an LLM reads the attachments, PDFs and engineering drawings included, and turns them into a structured specification. The system looks up similar past jobs through the ERP's API and drafts a reply with that context. Nothing is sent on its own: a person reads the draft, changes it if needed, and sends it.

The schedule

The shop's production schedule used to live in a spreadsheet. It now lives in the platform as a live schedule: one shared, current view of the work, inside the same platform as everything else. Quotes moved the same way, out of mail threads and into a quote workspace with a deal pipeline, and the reports people used to build by hand are now built-in analytics.
